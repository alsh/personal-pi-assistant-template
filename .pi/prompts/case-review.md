Review the private local case "$@".

Use pa_case_summary and document metadata. Report:

1. case status and due date;
2. open, waiting, done, and cancelled tasks;
3. linked documents and their hashes/freshness;
4. likely missing evidence only when supported by the user-provided checklist;
5. proposed next local tasks, without creating them automatically.

Do not submit, send, share, delete, or modify remote records.
