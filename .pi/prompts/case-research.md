Research the missing information for the local case or question "$@".

Workflow:

1. Read the case summary and existing local knowledge notes first.
2. State the jurisdiction and exact information gap.
3. Use public web research with privacy-safe queries; never include personal names,
   addresses, account numbers, serial numbers, or private document contents unless
   the user explicitly approves a specific source query.
4. Prefer official Polish/EU sources, regulators, legislation, provider terms, and
   the actual contract/invoice over blogs or generic summaries.
5. Use source_check/fetch_content for important claims and capture exact quotes,
   URLs, publisher, access date, confidence, and whether the source is binding or
   informational.
6. Separate verified facts, assumptions, unresolved questions, and proposed local
   tasks.
7. Ask for confirmation before saving the result with pa_record_knowledge or linking
   it to the case.

Do not give legal, tax, medical, or financial advice. Do not submit forms, send
messages, request a courier, make payments, or modify remote records.
